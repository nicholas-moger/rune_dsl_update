# Corpus licensing note

`builtins/` holds the two Rune DSL built-in model files (`basictypes.rosetta`, `annotations.rosetta`) copied from the FINOS Rune DSL distribution (Apache License 2.0). They are read-only inputs for the demo drivers and are never mutated or generated.

No other model corpus is committed under this directory. The CDM, ISO 20022 and DRR models the experiments were recorded on are acquired by the reviewer under their own licences at the pins recorded in `test-corpus/corpus-cells.tsv` and `docs/CORPUS-9.83.md`.
